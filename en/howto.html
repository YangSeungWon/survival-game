🎯 Objective

Survive the endless waves, level up, and defeat the boss that appears at level 15 (30,000 HP) to win (You Win!).

🎮 Controls

  • Move: arrow keys ↑ ↓ ← → (on-screen joystick on mobile).
  • Attack: automatic — auto-aims and fires at the nearest enemy.
  • Level-up: number keys 1 2 3 or click; Esc to cancel.

🔄 Core Loop

  • Killing enemies drops XP → walk over it to collect.
  • Fill the XP bar to level up → pick one power-up to grow stronger.
  • Higher levels spawn enemies faster and tougher.
  • Occasional hearts restore health.
  • At level 15 the boss appears → defeat it to clear.
